Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Users First, LLC, doing business as House Tandem ("we," "us," or "our"), operates the website housetandem.com and the games Tandemonium, 7, 11, Doubles, Roulette, Carnival Dozer and Timber Tandem. This policy explains what we collect and why. We don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

Website analytics (housetandem.com)

We count visits to this website with PostHog, a product analytics service, so we know which pages and links people use and where visitors come from.

  • No cookies, no stored identifiers. The website runs PostHog in its cookieless mode. Nothing that identifies you is saved in your browser. PostHog's servers count unique visitors with a one-way hash that cannot be turned back into who you are.
  • What is recorded: the pages you view, the page or campaign link that brought you here (for example a link from our TikTok or Instagram profile), which game or Steam links you click, and your browser and device type.
  • IP addresses are not kept. Analytics requests pass through our own server (e.housetandem.com, hosted on Cloudflare) on their way to PostHog, and our PostHog project is set to discard IP addresses.
  • Links to our games and to Steam carry tags (such as utm_source=housetandem) so we can see which pages lead people to a game. Steam reports those tags back to us only as totals.

Tandemonium

  • Game data: progress, achievements and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Gameplay analytics: to improve the game we record how it is played: session details (device type, screen size, input method and controller), ride results (level, completion, crashes, distance, frame rate), multiplayer connection quality, menu and tutorial steps, and clicks on links such as the Steam wishlist button. These records are tied to a random ID created on your device the first time you play, which lets us tell whether players come back. They are also stored with the IP address the request came from, which we use to filter out our own testing and to limit abuse. Analytics are stored in a Cloudflare database. We are moving gameplay analytics to PostHog; when we do, IP addresses will no longer be stored, and this page will be updated.
  • Google Sign-In (optional): if you sign in, we receive your display name and profile picture for the leaderboard. We do not access your email, contacts or other Google account data.
  • Steam: the Steam version uses Steam for sign-in and achievements under Steam's privacy policy.
  • Multiplayer: players connect directly to each other with WebRTC, or through our relay server when a direct connection isn't possible. Connection details (including IP addresses) pass through our servers to set up the connection and are not stored.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe or Steam. We never see or store card details. See stripe.com/privacy.

Discord Activities and other games

7, 11, Doubles, Roulette and Carnival Dozer each have their own privacy page, linked from the game. Timber Tandem currently collects no analytics. If we add analytics to any of them, it will work the way described on this page, and that game's page will say so first.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We use these service providers to run our games, and they process data only on our behalf: Cloudflare (hosting, game servers and databases), PostHog (analytics, US servers), GitHub (website hosting), Google (optional sign-in), Stripe and Steam (payments).

Children's privacy

Our games are family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

  • Clear your local game data at any time by clearing your browser's site data
  • Turn off analytics with the button above or with Global Privacy Control
  • Revoke Google Sign-In access in your Google account settings
  • Contact us to access or delete leaderboard or analytics data associated with you

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at games@usersfirst.com.
