    Delete your House Tandem account?

    This is permanent. Your account and every record on it are deleted straight away. We can't restore them, not even if you ask.

    This deletes:

    • Your account and its linked sign-ins: Discord and Steam
    • Every Tandemonium ride and leaderboard time (all)
    • Your achievements (all) and lifetime stats in every House Tandem game
    • Your time played and when you last played, in every game
    • Your Daily Ride results and streaks
    • Your records with riding partners. Their own rides stay, without your name.

    Not deleted: achievements on your Steam profile (those belong to Steam), and progress the game keeps on your own devices. If you play Tandemonium again and sign in, you'll start a new, empty account.
